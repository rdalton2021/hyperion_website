2023 FRC Season - Charged Up

This year's challenge, Charged Up 2023, is a game where you have to score cones in cube in a scoring grid.
The manipulation of these two game pieces can prove to be difficult because of their shapes and textures.
At the end of the game, the teams can attempt to balance on the Charging Station to collect more last-minute points.
